Startup Odisha Pitch

Odisha Offbeat

Discover the unseen Odisha. Swipe. Save. Travel. Empower locals.

A mobile-first discovery platform that surfaces hidden tourist destinations across Odisha and connects independent travelers with verified local hosts through curated packages.

Odisha Offbeat Live demo: odisha-offbeat.vercel.app
01 — Problem

Magnificent destinations remain invisible

Odisha's tourism is concentrated in Puri, Konark and Bhubaneswar. Districts like Koraput, Mayurbhanj, Kandhamal, Rayagada and Sundargarh hold spectacular, crowd-free places that travelers never find.

For travelers

  • Fragmented, unreliable information about attractions, transport, food and amenities
  • No centralized way to discover off-beat places
  • High friction to plan a trip to remote areas

For locals

  • Homestays, guides, transport and vendors have no digital reach
  • Income concentrated in peak season and famous spots
  • Young people migrate out for lack of opportunity
ProblemOdisha Offbeat
02 — Solution

Swipe to discover. Book to experience.

A Tinder-style swipe interface turns discovery into a 3-second decision. Each card is a destination; swipe right to save, left to pass. Tap the heart to reveal full details, essentials and host packages.

Discover

Curated destination cards with interest filters (Tribal, Beaches, Trekking, Wildlife, Heritage, Cuisine, Waterfalls, Eco).

Plan

Essentials layer: transport, stays, food, amenities. Host-curated packages with day-wise itineraries.

Book

One-tap booking request with a confirmation "aha moment" — itinerary, offline pack, share, calendar.

SolutionOdisha Offbeat
03 — Product

Two apps, one platform

Traveler app

  • 5-step onboarding personalizes the feed by travel style, interests, budget, trip length
  • Swipe deck with interest filters and a heart that opens full details
  • Destination detail: highlights, essentials, host packages, local hosts
  • Package detail with day-wise itinerary and inclusions
  • Dummy booking → confirmation with offline pack, share, calendar
  • Saved trips, map view, profile with bookings

Host app

  • Dashboard: listings, bookings, earnings
  • Create package: title, destination, price, duration, itinerary
  • Manage listings, view bookings, respond to travelers
  • Verified host badge (KYC + community references)
  • Profile & payout settings
