# Odisha Offbeat — Short Description

**One-liner:** A Tinder-style swipe discovery app that surfaces Odisha's hidden tourist destinations and lets travelers book host-curated packages with day-wise itineraries and local essentials — empowering rural communities while giving travelers authentic, centralized trip information.

## The idea in 3 sentences
Odisha has magnificent but obscure destinations (tribal belts, waterfalls, eco-zones) that travelers never discover because information is fragmented and unreliable. Odisha Offbeat turns discovery into a low-friction swipe experience: travelers swipe through curated destination cards, save favorites, and book packages curated by verified local hosts (homestays, guides, transport, vendors). Hosts get a dashboard to list packages and reach independent travelers directly, expanding rural economic reach.

## Target users
- **Travelers:** independent travelers, nature enthusiasts, cultural explorers seeking authentic off-beat experiences.
- **Hosts:** rural homestay owners, guides, transport providers, local vendors.

## Core features
- Swipe-to-discover destination cards with interest filters (Tribal, Beaches, Trekking, Wildlife, Heritage, Cuisine, Waterfalls, Eco)
- Traveler onboarding that personalizes the feed by travel style, interests, budget, trip length
- Destination detail with an "essentials" layer (transport, stays, food, amenities)
- Host-curated packages with day-wise itineraries and inclusions
- Dummy booking flow with a confirmation "aha moment" (itinerary, offline pack, share, calendar)
- Host dashboard to create/manage packages and track bookings
- Map view of all destinations; saved trips; traveler & host profiles

## Tech & cost
Next.js 16 + React + Tailwind, deployed on Vercel free tier. Zero-cost infra. Images local. Backend (Supabase free tier) planned for production. Live demo: https://odisha-offbeat.vercel.app

## Why it matters (Startup Odisha fit)
Aligns with Startup Odisha's goal of rural empowerment and inclusive growth: expands economic reach for homestay owners, guides, transport and vendors in tribal/remote districts, while centralizing reliable travel information for visitors.
